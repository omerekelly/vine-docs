This guide takes you from a new account to your first successful payment.

## Before you start

You need:

- A Nigerian business or freelancer account.
- An email address and phone number.
- Your BVN or a government ID for owner verification.
- For businesses, your CAC/RC details and director identification.
- A Nigerian bank account for payouts.

## Create your account

Go to [console.vine.ng/register](https://console.vine.ng/register) and sign up as a business or freelancer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Enter your name, email, phone number and password. Business accounts also need a business name and industry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete verification">
    Open **Settings → Compliance** and complete owner verification. Business accounts also complete business verification.

    <Note>
      Verification unlocks deposits and payouts. Your business name is locked after business verification is approved.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Live or Test">
    Use the switch in the top bar. Start in **Test** while you are setting things up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take your first payment">
    Create a [payment link](/payments/payment-links), a [hosted checkout](/payments/checkout) or an [invoice](/payments/invoices).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle to your bank">
    Link a payout account in **Wallet → Payout account**, then request a payout when you are ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>
