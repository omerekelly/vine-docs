Vine store plans cover your storefront. There are two plans, and each can be billed monthly or yearly from your workspace deposit balance.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Store Basic** | ₦1,500 | ₦15,000 | Opening a storefront and selling products, downloads or courses. |
| **Store Pro** | ₦3,500 | ₦35,000 | Stores that need premium themes, a custom domain and a free .com domain. |

Yearly billing costs 10 months and covers 12. Every workspace has its own plan.

## What is included

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Store Basic" icon="store">
    - Storefront at `your-business.vine.ng`
    - Shop products, digital downloads and courses
    - Orders, customers and discounts
    - Standard store themes
  </Card>

  <Card title="Store Pro" icon="crown">
    - Everything in Store Basic
    - Premium store themes
    - Connect your own custom domain
    - One free custom .com domain **on yearly billing only**
  </Card>

## Free .com domain

Store Pro **yearly** includes one free custom .com domain for the first year. Store Pro monthly does not include the domain, though you can still connect a domain you already own.

After subscribing to Store Pro yearly:

1. Open **Store settings → Domain**.
2. Enter the `.com` domain you want under **Claim your free .com domain**.
3. Submit the request. Vine registers the domain in your name and connects it to your storefront.

If you are on Store Pro monthly, the Domain page shows an upgrade prompt. Switch to yearly billing to claim the free domain.

## Choose or change your plan

1. In the dashboard, open **Store plans**.
2. Choose monthly or yearly billing.
2. Pick Store Basic or Store Pro.
3. Confirm the charge. The fee is taken from your deposit balance.

A plan change starts a new period immediately: 30 days for monthly, one year for yearly. Top up your deposit balance first if it is below the plan price.

Plans are prepaid and do not renew automatically. Renew from **Store plans** before the period ends.

<Note>
  Plan changes are dashboard-only because they debit your workspace deposit balance. The plan catalogue is available publicly at `GET /v1/plans`.
</Note>

## Plan catalogue API

`GET /v1/plans` is public and returns the active plans.

```bash
curl https://api.vine.ng/v1/plans
```

```json
{
  "plans": [
    {
      "slug": "starter",
      "name": "Store Basic",
      "priceMonthlyKobo": 150000,
      "priceYearlyKobo": 1500000,
      "premiumThemes": false,
      "customDomain": false,
      "freeDomain": false
    },
    {
      "slug": "pro",
      "name": "Store Pro",
      "priceMonthlyKobo": 350000,
      "priceYearlyKobo": 3500000,
      "premiumThemes": true,
      "customDomain": true,
      "freeDomain": true
    }
  ]
}
```

Amounts are in kobo. `150000` is ₦1,500.

The `freeDomain` flag marks the plan that can include a free domain. The benefit applies only when the subscription interval is `ANNUAL`.
