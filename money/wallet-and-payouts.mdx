Your Vine wallet holds the money you collect and gives you one place to move it.

## Balances

The wallet shows the balances available to your business. Collections from payment links, checkout, invoices and your storefront are recorded in the wallet.

Use the **Live / Test** switch to separate real balances from test activity. Test balances never settle to your bank.

## Link a payout account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payout">
    In the dashboard, go to **Payout** or open **Wallet → Payout account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your bank">
    Choose your bank and enter the account number. Vine resolves the account name before you save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with your PIN">
    Enter your transaction PIN to protect the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can change or unlink the account later. Payout account changes are protected by your transaction PIN.

## Request a payout

Choose the amount and send it to your linked bank account. Payouts are a flat **₦50 per transfer**.

<Note>
  Verification must be complete before deposits and payouts are available. See [Verification and compliance](/account/verification).
</Note>

## Transfers and bills

You can also move money from your wallet to another bank account or pay bills from the dashboard. Transfers from your Vine wallet cost **₦25** per transfer. Bill payments are fee-free.

## Transaction PIN

Your transaction PIN protects payouts, transfers and other money movements. Set it from the wallet if you have not created one yet. Keep it private; Vine will never ask you for it in a chat or email.
