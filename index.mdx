## What you can do with Vine

- **Collect payments** with payment links, hosted checkout and invoices.
- **Sell online** with a storefront, digital products and courses.
- **Move money** from your wallet to a Nigerian bank account.
- **Operate from one place** with customers, orders, discounts and transaction history.
- **Build on Vine** with API keys, webhooks and a documented API.

## Live and test mode

Use the **Live / Test** switch in the dashboard to change modes. Test mode lets you build and try flows without moving real money.

<Warning>
  Test payments do not settle to your bank account. Switch to Live before collecting from real customers.
</Warning>
