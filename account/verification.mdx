Vine verifies the account owner and, for business accounts, the registered business. Verification keeps deposits, payouts and collections secure.

## Owner verification

Owner verification confirms the identity of the person operating the account.

You will need:

- Your BVN or a government-issued ID.
- A selfie or liveness check if requested.
- Your name as it appears on your identity document.

Owner verification unlocks deposits and payouts.

## Business verification

Business accounts also verify the registered business.

You will need:

- Your CAC/RC registration details.
- The registered business name.
- Director identification and any documents requested in the flow.

<Note>
  Your business name is locked after business verification is approved. Contact support if the registered details need to change.
</Note>

## Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Not started | No verification has been submitted. |
| Pending | Submitted and waiting for review. |
| Approved | Verification is complete. |
| Rejected | The submission needs attention. Follow the instructions and resubmit. |

## Where to complete it

Open **Settings → Compliance** in the dashboard, or go to [console.vine.ng/kyc](https://console.vine.ng/kyc).

You can keep using test mode while verification is in progress. Live deposits and payouts require approval.

## Need help?

Email [support@vine.ng](mailto:support@vine.ng) with your business name and the email on your account.
