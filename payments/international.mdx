## What you can accept

| Method | Currencies | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International cards | USD | 4.5% + 40 cents |
| Mobile money (Ghana, Kenya) | GHS, KES | 2% |
| Mobile money (other African markets) | XAF, XOF, UGX, ZMW, TZS, RWF | 3% |
| Stablecoins | USDC, USDT on supported networks | 1.5% |

International cards include Visa, Mastercard and supported digital wallets. Mobile money includes M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money and other wallets available in the destination market.

## USD collections

USD card collections settle into your **USD collections** balance. Open **Wallet → Global collections** to see it. Mobile money balances are shown in the currency the customer paid.

## Payouts

Open **Payout** to withdraw USD collections:

- **Naira bank account:** converts USD to Naira at the current Vine rate and sends to your linked bank account.
- **US bank account:** sends USD by ACH to a US bank account, using the routing number, account type and address details you save once.
- **USDT wallet:** sends USDT to a wallet you link once, on Base or Ethereum.

## Pass fees to customers

In **Settings → Business → Processing fees**, enable **Pass fees to customers**. The processing fee is then added to the customer's checkout total, and you receive the full sale amount.

## Creating a USD checkout

Call `POST /v1/checkout/sessions` with `currency: "USD"`. The checkout page supports international cards, digital wallets, mobile money and stablecoins.

Public checkout links use the same format as Naira checkout.
