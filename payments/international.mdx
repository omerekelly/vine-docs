## What you can accept

| Method | Currencies | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| International cards | USD | 4.5% |
| Stablecoins | USDC, USDT on supported networks | 1% |

International cards include Visa, Mastercard and supported digital wallets.

## USD collections

USD card collections settle into your **USD collections** balance. Open **Wallet → Global collections** to see it.

## Payouts

Open **Payout** to withdraw USD collections:

- **Naira bank account:** converts USD to Naira at the current Vine rate and sends to your linked bank account.
- **USDT wallet:** sends USDT to a wallet you link once, on Base or Ethereum.

## Pass fees to customers

In **Settings → Business → Processing fees**, enable **Pass fees to customers**. The processing fee is then added to the customer's checkout total, and you receive the full sale amount.

## Creating a USD checkout

Call `POST /v1/checkout/sessions` with `currency: "USD"`. The checkout page supports international cards, digital wallets and stablecoins.

Public checkout links use the same format as Naira checkout.
