Hosted checkout is useful when you want a simple payment page without building your own form. Create a session, share the link, and Vine handles the payment experience.

## Create a checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Checkout">
    In the dashboard, go to **Checkout**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Add the amount in Naira. You can also add a customer email for the receipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick payment methods">
    Choose bank transfer, card, crypto, or a combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and share">
    Choose **Create checkout** and copy the hosted link. Send it to your customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment experience

The customer sees a Vine checkout page with your business name and the amount due. They can pay by:

- **Bank transfer:** Vine shows a dedicated account and watches for the transfer.
- **Card:** the customer enters their card details on Vine.
- **Crypto:** Vine shows the address, amount and supported network.

<Warning>
  Card details are encrypted in transit and are never stored by Vine. Do not collect card details outside the checkout.
</Warning>

## Test a checkout

Switch the dashboard to **Test** before creating a session if you want to try the flow without moving real money. Test payments do not settle to your bank account.

## Fees

Hosted checkout costs **1.5% per successful collection**, with a minimum fee of **₦10**. Failed payments are not charged.
